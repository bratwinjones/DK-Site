About DK

A family name behind
every degree of warmth.

DK began with a simple belief: a sauna or hot tub should be built once, built properly, and cared for like part of the family. Today our small team of certified installers and technicians delivers exactly that — precise installations, dependable repairs and meticulous maintenance for homes across Cape Town and the Western Cape.

From custom cedar cabins to fully fitted indoor saunas, we handle the whole journey: design advice, structural and electrical coordination, commissioning, and aftercare that keeps your room hot, your timber bright and your heater honest for years.

What we do

Services

Six ways DK keeps your wellness space perfect. Every job is quoted clearly and finished tidily.

Sauna Installation

Custom cedar, glass and stone saunas built to fit your space — bench layout, lighting and ventilation designed for real comfort.

Hot-Tub Installation

Level bases, clean plumbing and certified electrical connections. We position, commission and test your spa until every jet is right.

Repairs & Servicing

Heaters, controls, vents, seals and stalls — diagnosed with proper tools, fixed once, and explained in plain language before we touch anything.

Heater & Electrical Safety

Element replacements, thermostat calibration and certified inspections — wood-fired or electric, always isolated and lockout-safe.

Restoration & Upgrades

New panelling, benches, lighting and glass that make a tired sauna feel brand new — without replacing what still works.

Maintenance Plans

Scheduled servicing, stone replacement, seal checks and seasonal care so nothing surprises you — priority call-out for plan members.
